Xuhai Chen

I received my M.S. degree from Zhejiang University, China, in 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Yong Liu. Prior to that, I received my bachelor's degree from Tongji University, China.

In 2024, I interned at the State Key Laboratory of CAD&CG at Zhejiang University, where I worked on motion generation with Prof. Xiaowei Zhou and Prof. Sida Peng. In 2025, I worked as a remote research intern at the University of Pennsylvania, focusing on 3D reconstruction and generation with Prof. Lingjie Liu. Since July 2026, I have been collaborating remotely with Prof. Guandao Yang at UT Austin on 3D mesh generation and editing.

My research interests lie in 3D computer vision and graphics, with a particular focus on 3D generative modeling, reconstruction, and editing. I have also worked on image super-resolution and anomaly detection.

I enjoy exploring new experiences and challenges, which makes me feel happy and fulfilled. Life is meant to be experienced.
